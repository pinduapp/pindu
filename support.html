Pindu — Support

Pindu shows pinyin and English for Chinese text using your camera, photos, or typed/pasted text. Everything runs on your device.

Getting started

  • Camera tab: point at Chinese text to see pinyin and English. Tap any word for its reading, meaning, and to hear it spoken.
  • Text tab: type or paste Chinese text to read it.
  • Settings (gear icon) → “How to use” for a guided tour.

Pindu Premium

Pindu Premium (one-time purchase) adds Simplified⇄Traditional conversion, Closer Look (a stronger scan for hard pages), and saved readings with export. Already purchased? Settings → Restore Purchases.
