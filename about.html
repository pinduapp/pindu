About Pindu

Pindu (拼读, pīndú, “sound it out and read”) is an iPhone app for people who can already speak Chinese but were never taught to read it.

Why it exists

Plenty of people grew up speaking Chinese at home and never learned the characters, or learned a few hundred and lost them. Most apps aimed at Chinese assume you are starting from zero: lessons, flashcards, streaks. Camera translators go the other way and hand you English with no way to connect it to the sounds you already know.

Pindu sits in between. Point it at the page and it shows the pinyin above each word and the English below, then reads it aloud when you tap. You already know how the words sound. Pindu shows you which characters they are.

How it works

Everything happens on the phone. There is no server and no account, which is why the App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”. The pieces, in the order they run:

  • Reading the page. Apple's Vision framework recognises text in the live camera, in photos, and on frozen pages. Closer Look, the stronger scan, runs the PaddleOCR models on the phone through ONNX Runtime.
  • Pinyin and meanings. The CC-CEDICT dictionary, about 125,000 entries, is bundled inside the app. Pinyin is chosen for the word as a whole, so polyphones such as 银行 come out right.
  • English. Apple's Translation framework translates each sentence on the phone. The first time it runs, Apple downloads its Chinese to English language pack. On phones that support it, on-device Apple Intelligence picks the English word that matches each Chinese word in context.
  • Speech. Apple's built-in speech synthesis reads words, sentences, and whole pages using the Mandarin voices installed on your phone.

Pindu is made by one person. It is free to use, and a one-time purchase unlocks a few extras. There is no subscription, no advertising, and no analytics.
