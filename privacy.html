Privacy Policy for Pindu

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Pindu does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All processing happens entirely on your device. The text you photograph, import, type, or paste — and the camera images Pindu reads — are processed locally and are never sent to us or to any third party. Pindu has no user accounts, contains no advertising, includes no analytics or tracking, and has no servers. It functions offline, except that the first time you use English translation, Apple downloads a Chinese–English language pack over the internet; everything else works offline.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Pindu does not receive or store your payment information.

Pindu uses the following on-device Apple technologies, which run locally and do not send your content to us: Apple's Vision framework (text recognition), Apple's Translation framework, on-device Apple Intelligence (contextual word meanings), and Apple's speech synthesis (text-to-speech for reading words aloud).

Because Pindu collects no data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete.
