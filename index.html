Read what's in front of you.

Point the camera at a menu, a worksheet, or a bulletin. Open a screenshot of a relative's message. Or paste text. All three show the same reading: pinyin above, English below, a play button on every line.

  • Reads Simplified and Traditional characters
  • Pinch to zoom, up to 5x, with a macro lens button for small print
  • Tap the shutter to freeze a page and read at your own pace

Free

Tap a word. Hear it. Know it.

Tap 兔子 and you get tù zi, “rabbit”, and a voice that says it. Play reads the sentence from that word. Play all reads the whole page, one sentence at a time.

  • Pinyin is coloured by tone and taken from a 125,000-entry dictionary, so 银行 reads yín háng, not xíng
  • Hide the English with one tap when you want to read on your own
  • Natural Mandarin voices at the speed you choose

Tricky page? Hold for Closer Look.

Hold the shutter for a stronger scan of small print, dense pages, and Taiwanese kids' books with Zhuyin marks. It reads columns in the right order, straightens tilted shots, and dot-underlines any character it is unsure of so you can check and fix it.

  • Reads vertical text and two-column pages correctly
  • Tap a doubtful character to see other guesses or type the right one
  • About two seconds for a typical page

Read it in the script you know.

Switch any page between Simplified and Traditional. The conversion is phrase-aware, so 头发 becomes 頭髮, not 頭发, and words like 皇后 are left alone.

Keep it. Replay it. Share it.

Save a page and open it later with the pinyin, English, and playback intact. Copy the Chinese, the pinyin, the English, or all three, or share the reading as plain text.

  • Saved readings stay on your phone, not on a server
  • Swipe to delete when you are done with one

No account, no ads, no analytics, no server. The camera, the dictionary, the translation, and the voice all run on the iPhone itself, so the app works in airplane mode.
